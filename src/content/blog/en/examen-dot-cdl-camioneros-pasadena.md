The DOT exam is not meant to fail anyone: it is meant to confirm you can drive safely. Even so, many drivers leave with a short certificate or have to come back for a document. This guide, written for truck drivers, explains how to arrive prepared, what shortens the certificate and what to do afterward with DPS.

## How do I prepare to pass the first time?

- **Sleep well the night before and do not arrive rushed.** Stress and lack of sleep raise blood pressure, and blood pressure is the number one cause of short certificates.
- **Skip coffee, energy drinks and cigarettes in the two hours before**, for the same reason.
- **Take your medications as usual.** Stopping them "so it does not show on the exam" is what makes the exam go badly.
- **Bring your glasses or hearing aid.** The requirement is 20/40 vision in each eye and hearing a forced whisper at 5 feet, with correction allowed.
- **Use the restroom right before only if you already gave your urine sample.** The test looks for protein, blood or sugar; it is not a drug test.

## What shortens or denies the certificate?

The certificate lasts up to 24 months, but the medical examiner can issue it for less when something needs monitoring. The most frequent reasons:

1. **High blood pressure.** From 140/90 it usually means a one-year certificate; from 160/100, three months so you can get it under control; at 180/110 you are not certified until it comes down.
2. **Diabetes.** With pills and a good A1C you are certified; with insulin you need form MCSA-5870 completed by your treating physician within the last 45 days.
3. **Sleep apnea.** If you use a CPAP, bring the usage report; untreated, the examiner may require a sleep study before certifying.
4. **Uncorrected vision problems**, hearing loss without an aid, or a recent heart condition without cardiologist clearance.

## What documents do I bring?

A valid license, a medication list with doses, and depending on your case: a recent A1C result, the insulin form, the CPAP report, a letter from your cardiologist or specialist, and your previous certificate. If you have an FMCSA exemption (vision, diabetes or limbs), bring it too.

## What do I do with the certificate afterward?

In Texas, DPS requires you to file your medical certificate to keep your CDL active: it is submitted at a driver license office or through the DPS online portal along with your self-certification of driving type. If the certificate expires without renewal, the CDL can be downgraded to a regular class. Note the expiration date and schedule the renewal a month ahead.

## Can I come back if I did not pass?

Yes. If blood pressure was high, the reading can be repeated the same day or at a short visit after a few days of control. If a document was missing, the exam is completed when you bring it. The examiner tells you exactly what you need to be certified, and treatment for [high blood pressure or diabetes](/en/services/condiciones-cronicas) can start at the same clinic.
